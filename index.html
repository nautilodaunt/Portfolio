01 / ABOUT
IDENTITY // ARCHIVE BASED IN USA

Engineering thoughtful software at the intersection of clarity and depth.

I am a computer science student, software developer, and builder passionate about low-latency architectures, spatial computing, and editorial web experiences.

My work centers on building software that feels like well-bound books and precision mechanical instruments: quiet, reliable, durable, and free of unnecessary noise. I enjoy understanding systems from the ground up—from operating system primitives and network protocols down to typographic line height and micro-interactions.

What I Study

Computer Science, Algorithms & Complexity, Distributed Systems, and Operating Systems Architecture.

What I Build

Local-first applications, clean HTTP/gRPC APIs, developer tooling, and typography-rich web experiments.

Core Philosophy

Favor simplicity over abstraction, prioritize user ownership of data, and treat whitespace as a primary design material.

Interests & Pursuits

Marine biology, classical typography, mechanical keyboards, open-source tooling, and algorithmic geometry.
